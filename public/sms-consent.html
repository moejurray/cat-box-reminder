Cat Box Reminder — SMS Consent

This page documents the opt-in process for a private household SMS reminder system used by three family members.

Program purpose: Send a reminder to clean one household cat box approximately 36 hours after the previous cleaning. Messages are sent only between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM Pacific Time.

How consent is collected

Only invited members of the household are added to the reminder list. Before a phone number is entered into the system, that family member is shown the consent statement below and must explicitly agree to receive the messages.

SMS consent statement: I agree to receive recurring automated SMS reminders from the Cat Box Reminder system at the mobile number I provide. These messages relate only to cleaning our household cat box. Message frequency varies based on when the task is completed. Message and data rates may apply. I understand that I can reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Who receives messages

The notification list is limited to three members of one household. Phone numbers are not published on this page and are not shared or sold.

Message flow

After the cat box is marked cleaned, a 36-hour timer begins. When the timer expires, the household members receive an SMS reminder during the allowed sending window. When any recipient confirms that the box has been cleaned, the shared timer resets for another 36 hours.

Opt-out

Recipients may reply STOP to stop receiving messages. They may reply HELP for assistance. A household member may also ask the system administrator to remove their number at any time.

This is a private, non-commercial household reminder system. It does not advertise, market, sell, or promote any product or service.
